Eastern Europe · Specialist requirement

Financial Intelligence and Forensic Accounting Specialist

Hakluyt International is seeking an experienced financial intelligence practitioner for contract assignments supporting our Eastern European operations. The requirement sits at the intersection of forensic accounting, financial intelligence, sanctions analysis, illicit finance, and technical payment infrastructure.

Contract Remote Eastern Europe Kyiv / Vinnytsia verification

The requirement

We are interested in practitioners who can reconstruct the movement of money where the available record is incomplete, deliberately obscured, politically sensitive, or spread across multiple jurisdictions and payment systems.

The successful contractor should be comfortable moving between conventional accounting records, banking data, corporate structures, sanctions information, open source intelligence, payment infrastructure, and informal value transfer networks. The objective is not simply to identify an anomalous transaction. It is to explain the network, the mechanism, the counterparties, and the probable purpose behind it.

Previous work involving conflict finance, organized crime, sanctions evasion, corruption, defence procurement, asset tracing, or complex cross-border investigations is particularly relevant.

Core qualifications

  • Demonstrable professional experience in financial intelligence, forensic accounting, forensic audit, asset tracing, or complex financial investigations.
  • Strong working knowledge of AML and counter terrorist financing methodologies, FATF typologies, sanctions compliance, beneficial ownership analysis, and financial crime indicators.
  • Experience reconstructing layered transaction structures, nominee ownership, shell companies, intermediary networks, mule accounts, correspondent banking relationships, and cross-border movement of funds.
  • Previous exposure to war crimes investigations, conflict finance, military or defence related investigations, organized crime, corruption investigations, sanctions enforcement, or intelligence-led financial investigations is strongly preferred.
  • Ability to distinguish evidential findings from analytical inference and to document a financial trail to a standard suitable for scrutiny by legal, compliance, intelligence, or investigative professionals.
  • Familiarity with corporate registries, leaked datasets, sanctions lists, adverse media, procurement records, customs data, blockchain records, and other lawful OSINT sources.
  • Relevant professional credentials are desirable. These may include CFE, CAMS, CFCS, CPA/CA/ACCA, forensic accounting credentials, financial crime qualifications, or equivalent investigative experience.

Conflict and illicit finance

Informal value transfer

Practical experience identifying the use of hawala and hawala-like settlement networks, cash couriers, mirror transactions, trade settlement, informal remittance channels, underground banking, and other systems capable of moving value without a conventional end-to-end banking trail.

Conflict-zone typologies

Familiarity with sanctions circumvention, intermediary jurisdictions, procurement fronts, cash-intensive businesses, commodity settlement, cryptocurrency, payment agents, high-risk exchange networks, and organized-crime-linked financial structures encountered in Eastern Europe and the former Soviet space.

Network analysis

Ability to move from individual transactions to a defensible assessment of relationships between people, companies, accounts, wallets, intermediaries, jurisdictions, and commercial activity.

Source discipline

Candidates should be comfortable working with imperfect information and should know when a pattern is significant, when it is merely unusual, and when the available evidence does not support a conclusion.

Technical background

Backend development experience is not mandatory, but it is highly desirable. We are particularly interested in candidates who understand financial systems well enough to work directly with engineers rather than treating payment infrastructure as a black box.

  • Experience developing or integrating custom APIs, transaction monitoring systems, reconciliation tools, financial data pipelines, or investigative analytics platforms.
  • Familiarity with ISO 20022 financial messaging and structured payment data.
  • Practical understanding of SWIFT, correspondent banking, IBAN based payments, SEPA payment infrastructure, and cross-border settlement.
  • Familiarity with Canadian payment infrastructure, including Payments Canada, is strongly desirable.
  • Familiarity with United Kingdom payment infrastructure, including systems operated by or associated with Pay.UK, is strongly desirable.
  • Experience querying large financial datasets using SQL, Python, APIs, graph tools, or equivalent analytical systems is advantageous.

Language and location

Languages

Minimum professional working proficiency of B2 English is required. Candidates should also have at least B1 proficiency in either Ukrainian or Russian, with working ability in both strongly preferred.

Location

The work is primarily remote. Successful candidates must, however, be able to attend an in-person meeting in Kyiv or Vinnytsia, Ukraine for identity, qualification, and credential verification before undertaking sensitive assignments.

Engagement

This is not a salaried generalist position. Engagements are contract based and assigned according to subject matter, jurisdiction, availability, and the requirements of individual matters.

Some assignments may be narrow and analytical. Others may require sustained reconstruction of financial activity across multiple entities, jurisdictions, languages, and data sources.

Applicants should be capable of working independently, maintaining disciplined records, handling sensitive commercial information, and producing concise written findings for senior decision makers.

Expression of interest

Initial correspondence should be concise. Send a curriculum vitae or professional summary together with your principal areas of financial intelligence experience, relevant jurisdictions, languages, technical capabilities, and professional qualifications.

Do not send classified information, protected investigative material, client records, operational files, or evidence from previous engagements.

Suitable candidates will be contacted directly. References and credentials may be independently verified before engagement.
